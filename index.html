Danny’s bio

In brief
Bestselling author of 14 books on creativity and drawing. Founder of SketchbookSkool.com. 500K+ YouTube subscribers. Former Executive Creative Director. Phoenix, AZ.
In detail
Danny Gregory is an internationally bestselling author of 14 books on art and creativity. As founder of Sketchbook Skool, he has inspired 50,000+ students worldwide to discover their artistic abilities. His YouTube channel features video essays and drawing lessons that reach over 500,000 subscribers. He publishes weekly essays to 30,000+ readers at dannysessays.com. Danny spent three decades as one of New York’s leading advertising creative directors, creating award-winning global campaigns. He was born in London, grew up in Pakistan, Australia, Israel, and New York City, and graduated summa cum laude from Princeton University. He now lives in Phoenix, AZ.
